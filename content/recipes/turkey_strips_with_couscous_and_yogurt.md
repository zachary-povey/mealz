1. Steam the {{< ingredient_mention name="courgette" fraction="1.0" >}} until soft, then blend it into the hot {{< ingredient_mention name="vegetable stock" fraction="1.0" >}}.
2. Pour the stock over the {{< ingredient_mention name="couscous" fraction="1.0" >}}, cover and leave for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
3. Slice the {{< ingredient_mention name="turkey breast" fraction="1.0" >}} into thin strips and toss with the {{< ingredient_mention name="ground cumin" fraction="1.0" >}}. Heat the {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="1.0" >}} in a frying pan and cook the strips for 5 to 6 minutes until cooked through.
4. Grate the {{< ingredient_mention name="carrot" fraction="1.0" >}} and serve it in a small pile next to the turkey and couscous, with the {{< ingredient_mention name="natural yogurt" fraction="1.0" >}} for dipping.
