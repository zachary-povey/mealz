1. Peel the {{< ingredient_mention name="courgette" fraction="1.0" >}} so there are no green flecks, then grate it on the fine side of the grater. Squeeze out as much water as you can in a clean tea towel.
2. Finely chop the {{< ingredient_mention name="cooked ham" fraction="1.0" >}}.
3. Mix the {{< ingredient_mention name="plain flour" fraction="1.0" >}} and {{< ingredient_mention name="baking powder" fraction="1.0" >}} in a bowl. Add the {{< ingredient_mention name="eggs" fraction="1.0" >}} and {{< ingredient_mention name="milk" fraction="1.0" >}} and whisk to a thick, smooth batter.
4. Stir in the courgette, ham and grated {{< ingredient_mention name="cheese" fraction="1.0" >}}. The batter should be thick enough to hold its shape on a spoon.
5. Heat a non-stick frying pan over a medium-low heat, lightly oiling it with the {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="1.0" >}} before each batch. Add heaped tablespoons of batter, leaving space between them, and cook for 2–3 minutes until bubbles appear on top and the underside is golden.
6. Flip and cook for another 2–3 minutes until risen and cooked through. Repeat with the rest of the batter and serve warm.
