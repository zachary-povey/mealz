1. Cook the {{< ingredient_mention name="long grain brown rice" fraction="1.0" >}} as per the packet instructions, ideally the day before. Drain well, spread it out on a tray to cool quickly, then refrigerate within an hour. Cold, dry rice fries without going clumpy.
2. Peel and finely dice the {{< ingredient_mention name="carrot" fraction="1.0" >}}. Finely chop the {{< ingredient_mention name="garlic" fraction="1.0" >}} and peel and grate the {{< ingredient_mention name="fresh ginger" fraction="1.0" >}}.
3. Beat the {{< ingredient_mention name="eggs" fraction="1.0" >}} in a jug. Heat {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="0.25" >}} in a wok or large frying pan over a medium-high heat, pour in the eggs and scramble until just set. Tip onto a plate.
4. Turn the heat up to high, add the remaining {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="0.75" >}} and stir-fry the carrot for 3 minutes until starting to soften.
5. Add the garlic and ginger and stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant, without letting them brown.
6. Add the {{< ingredient_mention name="frozen peas" fraction="1.0" >}} and the cold rice, breaking up any clumps, and stir-fry for 4–5 minutes until the rice is piping hot and starting to crisp in places.
7. Stir in the {{< ingredient_mention name="low salt soy sauce" fraction="1.0" >}} and return the eggs to the pan, breaking them into small pieces. Toss for a minute and serve.
