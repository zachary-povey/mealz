1. Cut the {{< ingredient_mention name="cauliflower" fraction="1.0" >}} into florets and boil for 8 to 10 minutes until soft, then drain.
2. Melt the {{< ingredient_mention name="butter" fraction="1.0" >}} in a saucepan, stir in the {{< ingredient_mention name="plain flour" fraction="1.0" >}} and cook for a minute. Gradually whisk in the {{< ingredient_mention name="milk" fraction="1.0" >}} and simmer for 3 minutes until thickened.
3. Stir {{< ingredient_mention name="cheese" fraction="0.75" >}} into the sauce, then blend in a handful of the cauliflower florets.
4. Put the rest of the florets in an ovenproof dish that fits in the air fryer basket, pour over most of the sauce, scatter over the remaining {{< ingredient_mention name="cheese" fraction="0.25" >}} and air fry at 200°C for 5 minutes until golden. Do it in two batches if it doesn't all fit.
5. Meanwhile cook the {{< ingredient_mention name="wholewheat pasta" fraction="1.0" >}} as per the packet instructions, drain and toss it through the leftover sauce to serve alongside.
