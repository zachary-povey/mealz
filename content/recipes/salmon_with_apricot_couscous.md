1. Cut the {{< ingredient_mention name="courgette" fraction="1.0" >}} and {{< ingredient_mention name="red pepper" fraction="1.0" >}} into small chunks, toss with {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="0.75" >}} and air fry at 200°C for 12–15 minutes, shaking halfway, until tender and starting to char. Tip into a bowl.
2. Drizzle the {{< ingredient_mention name="salmon fillet" fraction="1.0" >}} with the remaining {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="0.25" >}} and air fry at 200°C for 8–10 minutes until it flakes easily.
3. Meanwhile, finely chop the {{< ingredient_mention name="dried apricots" fraction="1.0" >}} and put them in a bowl with the {{< ingredient_mention name="couscous" fraction="1.0" >}}. Pour over the hot {{< ingredient_mention name="vegetable stock" fraction="1.0" >}}, cover and leave for 5 minutes.
4. Fluff the couscous with a fork, stir through the vegetables and squeeze over the {{< ingredient_mention name="lemon" fraction="1.0" >}}.
5. Flake the salmon, checking for bones, and serve it on top of the couscous.
