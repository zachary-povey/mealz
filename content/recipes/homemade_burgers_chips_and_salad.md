1. Peel the {{< ingredient_mention name="potatoes" fraction="1.0" >}} if you like and cut them into chips about 1 cm thick. Rinse them in cold water, then dry them thoroughly on a clean tea towel.
2. Toss the chips with {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="0.5" >}} and a pinch of {{< ingredient_mention name="salt" fraction="0.5" >}}. Air fry at 200°C for 20–25 minutes, shaking the basket every 5 minutes, until golden and crisp. Cook them in batches if needed so the basket is no more than half full, then put them all back in for the last 2–3 minutes to heat through.
3. Meanwhile, mix the {{< ingredient_mention name="chicken mince" fraction="1.0" >}} with a pinch of {{< ingredient_mention name="salt" fraction="0.25" >}} and {{< ingredient_mention name="black pepper" fraction="1.0" >}}. With wet hands, shape it into one patty per serving, about 1.5 cm thick. Chill until ready to cook.
4. Heat {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="0.25" >}} in a non-stick frying pan over a medium heat and cook the burgers for 5–6 minutes on each side until cooked through, with no pink in the middle and the juices running clear. Lightly toast the cut sides of the {{< ingredient_mention name="burger buns" fraction="1.0" >}} in the pan.
5. Just before serving, tip the {{< ingredient_mention name="four seasons salad" fraction="1.0" >}} into a bowl and toss with the remaining {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="0.25" >}} and a pinch of {{< ingredient_mention name="salt" fraction="0.25" >}}.
6. Serve the burgers in the buns with the chips and salad on the side.
