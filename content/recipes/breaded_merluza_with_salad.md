1. Defrost the {{< ingredient_mention name="frozen merluza portions" fraction="1.0" >}} in the fridge, ideally overnight, then pat them dry with kitchen paper.
2. Beat the {{< ingredient_mention name="eggs" fraction="1.0" >}} with a pinch of {{< ingredient_mention name="salt" fraction="0.5" >}} in a shallow bowl and put the {{< ingredient_mention name="breadcrumbs" fraction="1.0" >}} on a plate. Dip each portion in the egg, then press it into the breadcrumbs to coat all over.
3. Drizzle the fish with {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="0.5" >}} and air fry at 200°C for 10–12 minutes, turning halfway, until the crumb is golden and the fish flakes easily. Cook in batches if they don't fit in a single layer.
4. Meanwhile, halve the {{< ingredient_mention name="cherry tomatoes" fraction="1.0" >}} (quarter them for little ones), dice the {{< ingredient_mention name="cucumber" fraction="1.0" >}} and slice the {{< ingredient_mention name="olives" fraction="1.0" >}}. Toss together with the remaining {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="0.5" >}} and a pinch of {{< ingredient_mention name="salt" fraction="0.5" >}}.
5. Serve the fish with the salad on the side.
