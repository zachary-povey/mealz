1. Preheat the oven to 200°C. Put the {{< ingredient_mention name="salmon fillet" fraction="1.0" >}} on a lined tray, drizzle with the {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="1.0" >}} and squeeze over the {{< ingredient_mention name="lemon" fraction="1.0" >}}. Bake for 12 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, very finely grate the {{< ingredient_mention name="carrot" fraction="1.0" >}} into a bowl with the {{< ingredient_mention name="couscous" fraction="1.0" >}}, pour over the hot {{< ingredient_mention name="vegetable stock" fraction="1.0" >}}, cover and leave for 5 minutes.
3. Cook the {{< ingredient_mention name="frozen peas" fraction="1.0" >}} in boiling water for 3 minutes and drain.
4. Fluff the couscous with a fork and stir through the {{< ingredient_mention name="butter" fraction="1.0" >}}.
5. Serve the salmon flaked over the couscous, with the peas on the side.
