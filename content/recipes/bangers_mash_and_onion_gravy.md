1. Halve and thinly slice the {{< ingredient_mention name="onion" fraction="1.0" >}}. Heat the {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="1.0" >}} in a frying pan over a medium-low heat and cook the onions for 15–20 minutes, stirring often, until soft and golden.
2. Meanwhile, peel the {{< ingredient_mention name="floury potatoes" fraction="1.0" >}} and cut them into even chunks. Put them in a pan of cold water with a pinch of {{< ingredient_mention name="salt" fraction="1.0" >}}, bring to the boil and simmer for 15–20 minutes until very tender.
3. Air fry the {{< ingredient_mention name="pork sausages" fraction="1.0" >}} at 180°C for 12–15 minutes, turning halfway, until browned and cooked through with no pink in the middle.
4. Stir the {{< ingredient_mention name="plain flour" fraction="1.0" >}} into the onions and cook for a minute. Dissolve the {{< ingredient_mention name="beef stock cube" fraction="1.0" >}} in the hot {{< ingredient_mention name="water" fraction="1.0" >}} and gradually stir it in, then simmer for 3–5 minutes until thickened.
5. Cook the {{< ingredient_mention name="frozen peas" fraction="1.0" >}} in boiling water for 3 minutes and drain.
6. Drain the potatoes, leave them to steam for a minute, then mash with the {{< ingredient_mention name="milk" fraction="1.0" >}} and {{< ingredient_mention name="butter" fraction="1.0" >}} until smooth.
7. Serve the sausages on the mash with the onion gravy poured over and the peas on the side.
