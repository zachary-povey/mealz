1. Put the {{< ingredient_mention name="onion" fraction="1.0" >}} in the Thermomix bowl and chop for 5 sec/speed 5. Scrape down the sides.
2. Add the {{< ingredient_mention name="courgette" fraction="1.0" >}} in chunks, the {{< ingredient_mention name="water" fraction="1.0" >}}, {{< ingredient_mention name="chicken stock cube" fraction="1.0" >}}, {{< ingredient_mention name="salt" fraction="1.0" >}} and {{< ingredient_mention name="ground black pepper" fraction="1.0" >}}. Cook for 20 min/100°C/speed 1.
3. Add the {{< ingredient_mention name="butter" fraction="1.0" >}} and {{< ingredient_mention name="cream cheese" fraction="1.0" >}} and blend for 1 min/speed 5-10, increasing the speed gradually, until smooth. Add a little more water if you prefer it thinner.
