1. Peel and dice the {{< ingredient_mention name="butternut squash" fraction="1.0" >}}, steam it for 12 minutes until soft, then blend it to a smooth purée.
2. Cook the {{< ingredient_mention name="wholewheat macaroni" fraction="1.0" >}} as per the packet instructions and drain.
3. Melt the {{< ingredient_mention name="butter" fraction="1.0" >}} in a saucepan, stir in the {{< ingredient_mention name="plain flour" fraction="1.0" >}} and cook for a minute. Gradually whisk in the {{< ingredient_mention name="milk" fraction="1.0" >}} and simmer for 3 minutes until thickened.
4. Stir {{< ingredient_mention name="cheddar" fraction="0.75" >}} and the squash purée into the sauce, then stir in the macaroni.
5. Tip into an ovenproof dish, scatter over the remaining {{< ingredient_mention name="cheddar" fraction="0.25" >}} and grill for 3 to 4 minutes until golden.
6. Cook the {{< ingredient_mention name="frozen peas" fraction="1.0" >}} in boiling water for 3 minutes and serve them on the side.
