1. Steam the {{< ingredient_mention name="cauliflower" fraction="1.0" >}} until very soft, then blend it to a smooth purée.
2. Melt the {{< ingredient_mention name="butter" fraction="1.0" >}} in a saucepan, stir in the {{< ingredient_mention name="plain flour" fraction="1.0" >}} and cook for a minute. Gradually whisk in the {{< ingredient_mention name="milk" fraction="1.0" >}} and simmer for 3 minutes until thickened.
3. Take the sauce off the heat and stir in the grated {{< ingredient_mention name="cheddar" fraction="1.0" >}} and the cauliflower purée.
4. Cook the {{< ingredient_mention name="gnocchi" fraction="1.0" >}} in boiling water for 2 minutes, until they float, and steam the {{< ingredient_mention name="broccoli" fraction="1.0" >}} florets at the same time.
5. Drain the gnocchi, toss them in the cheese sauce and serve with the broccoli on the side.
