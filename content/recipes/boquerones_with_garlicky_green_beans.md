1. Buy the {{< ingredient_mention name="boquerones" fraction="1.0" >}} cleaned and opened with the spine removed ("limpios y abiertos"); the fishmonger will do it. Rinse them and pat them very dry with kitchen paper.
2. Season the fish with {{< ingredient_mention name="salt" fraction="0.5" >}}, toss in the {{< ingredient_mention name="harina para freír" fraction="1.0" >}} and shake off the excess in a sieve.
3. Lay the fish in a single layer in the lightly oiled air fryer basket and brush or spray with {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="0.5" >}}. Air fry at 200°C for 8–10 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and crisp. Cook in batches.
4. Meanwhile, top and tail the {{< ingredient_mention name="green beans" fraction="1.0" >}} and cut them in half. Boil in water with a pinch of {{< ingredient_mention name="salt" fraction="0.25" >}} for 6–8 minutes until tender, then drain.
5. Thinly slice the {{< ingredient_mention name="garlic" fraction="1.0" >}}. Gently heat the remaining {{< ingredient_mention name="olive oil" fraction="0.5" >}} in the empty pan, cook the garlic for a minute without letting it brown, then toss the beans in it with a pinch of {{< ingredient_mention name="salt" fraction="0.25" >}}.
6. Serve the fish with the {{< ingredient_mention name="lemon" fraction="1.0" >}} in wedges and the beans on the side.
