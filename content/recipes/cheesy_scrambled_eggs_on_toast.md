1. Steam the {{< ingredient_mention name="cauliflower" fraction="1.0" >}} until very soft, then blend it to a smooth purée.
2. Whisk the {{< ingredient_mention name="eggs" fraction="1.0" >}} with the {{< ingredient_mention name="milk" fraction="1.0" >}}, the cauliflower purée and the grated {{< ingredient_mention name="cheddar" fraction="1.0" >}}.
3. Melt {{< ingredient_mention name="butter" fraction="0.5" >}} in a non-stick pan over a low heat, add the egg mixture and stir gently until just set.
4. Toast the {{< ingredient_mention name="wholemeal bread" fraction="1.0" >}}, spread it with the remaining {{< ingredient_mention name="butter" fraction="0.5" >}} and cut it into fingers.
5. Serve the eggs with the toast fingers and a few batons of {{< ingredient_mention name="cucumber" fraction="1.0" >}} on the side.
