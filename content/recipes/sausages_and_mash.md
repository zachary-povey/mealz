1. Air fry the {{< ingredient_mention name="turkey sausages" fraction="1.0" >}} at 180°C for 12 to 15 minutes, turning halfway, until cooked through with no pink in the middle.
2. Peel and chop the {{< ingredient_mention name="floury potatoes" fraction="1.0" >}} and boil for 15 minutes, adding the {{< ingredient_mention name="cauliflower" fraction="1.0" >}} florets for the last 10 minutes, until both are very soft.

4. Cook the {{< ingredient_mention name="frozen peas" fraction="1.0" >}} in boiling water for 3 minutes and serve them beside the sausages and mash.
